# Drone Racing League

> The Drone Racing League (DRL) is a New York-based professional drone racing property founded in 2015 by Nicholas Horbaczewski. Pilots fly identical, league-built FPV quadcopters (the Racer series) through three-dimensional courses in stadiums, arenas and landmark venues at speeds above 80 mph. DRL designs its own drones, timing and telemetry hardware, produces its race broadcasts for NBC, Sky Sports and ProSiebenSat.1, and operates the DRL Simulator on Steam, PlayStation and Xbox, which feeds the Swatch DRL Tryouts esports pipeline used to recruit professional pilots. DRL was acquired by Infinite Reality (now Napster) in April 2024. **DRL publishes no public API, developer portal, SDK, or machine-readable specification.**
